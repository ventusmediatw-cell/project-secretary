一句話：skill 生態只有一個門——進門先分三條路，所有產出都停在「請你拍板」。
三條路 · 五份 SOP。下面 3 張圖就是全部機制。 One sentence: the skill ecosystem has a single door — three routes branch inside it, and every outcome stops at "your call".
3 routes · 5 SOPs. The 3 diagrams below are the whole machine.

圖一 · 進門先分路Fig 1 · Route at the door

一進來就分路；不確定走哪條，它會問你、不自行認定。Routing happens first; when unsure, it asks you — it never guesses.

為什麼要這樣設計Why it's built this way

三條路終點相同：模型有「想做點事證明有用」的偏見，所以拍板權永遠留在你手上。 All three routes share one endpoint: models are biased toward doing something to prove useful, so the final call always stays with you.

圖二 · 想建新的？先過兩道閘Fig 2 · New skill? Two gates first

掃兩層才准動手：本地既有 skill ＋ 官方現成的（同一次只掃一遍）。Two scans before any work: local skills + official offerings (scanned once per visit).

為什麼要這樣設計Why it's built this way

多數想抽的其實不該抽——兩道閘先擋掉重造輪子和一時衝動，擋剩的才值得花工建。 Most skill ideas shouldn't become skills — two gates stop wheel-reinventing and impulse-building; what survives is worth the work.

圖三 · 兩條反向的路Fig 3 · Two roads, opposite directions

你找它，或它自己跳出來提醒你——最後都走進同一顆菱形。You call it, or it nudges you — both roads end at the same diamond.

為什麼要這樣設計Why it's built this way

人會忘、hook 不會；但 hook 只負責喊，跑不跑仍由你拍板——主動面與決定權分開。 People forget; hooks don't. But the hook only shouts — whether to run is still your call. Initiative and authority stay separate.

反過度封裝Anti over-build

  • 先找既有的，找不到才自製search existing first; build only if none

  • AI 只能提案，不能自己改檔AI proposes, never edits on its own
  • 連加「豁免標記」都要你點頭even exemption tags need your nod
