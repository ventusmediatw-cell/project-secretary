你的 AI
聽不見聲音。

Your AI
can't hear sound.

而且它不會說。

你把錄音丟給它，它不會報錯。它會看檔名，寫出一份讀起來很像樣的摘要——那份摘要是編的。

And it won't say so.

Hand it a recording and you get no error. It looks at the filename and writes a summary that reads just fine — the summary is made up.

所以，讓另一支
程式去聽。

它叫 agy。它真的聽得到聲音，把聲音寫成字。你的 AI 讀那些字。

So let another
program listen.

It is called agy. It really can hear, and it writes the sound down. Your AI reads what it wrote.

叫它裝一次就好。

你的 AI 會自己把負責聽的那支程式裝好。等瀏覽器跳出來，你用 Google 帳號登入一次——這是唯一要你親手做的事。之後你只要把檔案交出去，再說一句誰在講話。

Tell it to install once.

Your AI installs the program that does the listening by itself. When a browser window opens, you sign in with your Google account once — that is the only step that needs your hands. After that you just hand over the file and say who was in the room.

你拿回兩樣東西。

一句一句的逐字稿，還有聲音裡的線索——誰插了話、哪裡靜了下來、誰在笑。這些，字面上看不出來。

You get back
two things.

The words, sentence by sentence — and the clues in the sound: who cut in, where it went quiet, who laughed. None of that shows up in the text.

名字、數字和日期
最容易錯。

錯的那個字，長得跟對的一模一樣。先回你自己的資料查過，再拿去用。

Names, numbers and dates
break first.

A wrong word looks exactly like a right one. Look each one up in your own records before you use it.

錄音跟逐字稿
放在一起。

它每次聽出來的都不太一樣。哪句話有爭議的時候，只有原始錄音能回頭查。同一個名字、同一個資料夾。

Keep the recording
next to the transcript.

It never hears it quite the same way twice. When a line is disputed, the audio is the only thing to go back to. Same name, same folder.
