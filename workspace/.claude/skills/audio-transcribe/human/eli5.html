你的 AI
聽不見聲音。

Your AI
can't hear sound.

而且它不會說。

你把錄音丟給它，它不會報錯。它會看檔名，寫出一份讀起來很像樣的摘要——那份摘要是編的。

And it won't say so.

Hand it a recording and you get no error. It looks at the filename and writes a summary that reads just fine — the summary is made up.

所以先把聲音
變成字。

另一支程式負責聽，它把聲音寫成一份逐字稿。AI 讀那份逐字稿。

So turn the sound
into text first.

Another program does the listening. It writes the sound down as a transcript. The AI reads that transcript.

一條命令。

bash tools/transcribe.sh recording.m4a zh

最後那個是語言：英文 en、中文 zh、高棉語 km。進去什麼語言，出來就是什麼語言——它不翻譯。

One command.

bash tools/transcribe.sh recording.m4a en

The last part is the language: en for English, zh for Chinese, km for Khmer. What goes in comes out in the same language — it does not translate.

名字、數字和日期
最容易錯。

錯的那幾個字，看起來跟對的一模一樣。寄出去、貼進共用文件之前，自己核一次。

Names, numbers and dates
break first.

A wrong word looks exactly like a right one. Check them yourself before you send it out or paste it into a shared document.
