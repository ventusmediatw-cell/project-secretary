對著電腦講話，
它幫你打字。

You talk to your computer.
It types for you.

而且不用上網。
整件事就在你的桌上發生。

It never goes online.
The whole thing happens on your desk.

只有一個麻煩：
它學中文的時候，學到的是簡體。

字是對的意思，但不是台灣人講話的樣子。

One catch:
the Chinese it learned is simplified.

The meaning is right. It is just not how people write in Taiwan.

解法：
給它一張台灣話小抄。

兩張都是複製、貼上、存檔。不用寫程式，也還是不用上網。

The fix:
give it a Taiwan cheat sheet.

Both are copy, paste, save. No code, and still no internet.

貼完之後，
它就開始講台灣話。

不會百分之百完美——偶爾還是會漏一兩個簡體字，自己改掉就好。

Paste those in,
and it starts writing like Taiwan.

It will not be perfect. A simplified character slips through now and then, and you fix it by hand.
