In one line: you speak, Whisper turns it into text on your Mac (nothing goes online); the text leans simplified and mainland-style, so you paste in two pieces to pull it back to Taiwan Chinese.
The three diagrams below are the whole mechanism.

圖一 · 兩件套怎麼把字掰回來

示範文在轉字「之前」讀，字典在轉字「之後」換

為什麼要兩件一起貼

示範文負責把整體拉到繁體字形，字典才抓得到；只貼字典，簡體那條路直接漏網。

Diagram 1 · How the two pieces pull the text back

The sample text is read before transcription; the dictionary swaps words after it

Why you paste both

The sample text pulls the whole output into traditional characters, which is the only way the dictionary can match. Paste the dictionary alone and the simplified path slips straight through.

圖二 · 要不要再請一個本機 AI

三條建議路線都在你的 Mac 上跑完，差別只在品質、速度、安裝門檻

怎麼選

夠用就停在路線一；要最好的品質、也願意每句多等一下本機 AI，才裝 Ollama。

Diagram 2 · Do you also need a local AI?

All three recommended routes finish on your Mac; they differ only in quality, speed, and setup effort

How to choose

If it is good enough, stop at Route 1. Install Ollama only when you want the best quality and accept a short wait on every line.

圖三 · 中英夾雜的陷阱

示範文的引導力強到會把整句英文翻成中文

實測

唸「I think this application is very elegant」，語言鎖定中文時輸出的是「我覺得這個應用程式很優雅。」；同一段錄音改 Auto-detect 就保留英文（實測記錄日期 2026-06-27）。改 Auto-detect 是緩解、不是根治——要根治就把繁體化整個交給路線二的本機 Ollama。

Diagram 3 · The trap when you mix in English

The sample text steers so hard that a whole English sentence comes back in Chinese

Measured

Say “I think this application is very elegant” with the language locked to Chinese and what lands is 「我覺得這個應用程式很優雅。」 — the Chinese translation. The same recording under Auto-detect keeps the English (tested 2026-06-27). Auto-detect eases the symptom rather than fixing it; the real fix is to hand the whole traditional-character job to the local Ollama in Route 2.

三個要先知道的事

順序不能反
  • 示範文是「示範」不是命令，在裡面寫「請輸出繁體中文」沒有用。
  • 它在轉字之前被讀，字典在轉字之後才換。
合理期望：大致繁體＝夠用
  • 絕大多數輸出是台灣中文，偶爾漏一兩個簡體字屬正常。
  • 設定包是調教，不是換一顆腦。
這條管線不做什麼
  • 不做翻譯：英文被翻成中文是副作用，不是功能。
  • 不會自己上網：三條建議路線全部離線；唯一會把文字送出 Mac 的是雲端 enhancement，而它預設是關的（2026-06 稽核結論）。

Three things to know first

The order cannot be reversed
  • The sample text is a sample, not a command. Writing “please output traditional Chinese” inside it does nothing.
  • It is read before transcription; the dictionary only swaps words afterwards.
A fair expectation: mostly traditional is enough
  • Nearly all output is Taiwan Chinese. One or two simplified characters slipping through is normal.
  • The settings tune the model; they do not swap in a different brain.
What this pipeline does not do
  • No translation: English coming back as Chinese is a side effect, not a feature.
  • No going online by itself: all three recommended routes stay offline. The only thing that sends text off the Mac is cloud enhancement, and it is off by default (audit, 2026-06).
