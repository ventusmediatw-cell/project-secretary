你問：
這東西怎麼運作？

它不寫一大篇給你。它畫圖。

You ask:
how does this work?

It doesn't write you an essay. It draws.

它先找
哪裡會分岔。

一條路講到底，聽起來很順，但「什麼時候會不一樣」全被藏起來了。所以它先把岔路挑出來，再開始畫。

It looks for
the forks first.

A story told in a straight line sounds smooth, but it hides every place where things could have gone the other way. So it picks out the forks before it draws.

最多三張圖。
字很少。

三張講不完，表示題目太大了——先切小，再畫。字只是配角。

Three pictures, at most.
Very few words.

If three aren't enough, the subject is too big — cut it down, then draw. The words are only the support act.

顏色是有意思的。

四個顏色，三張圖都用同一套。看顏色就知道那一格是什麼。

The colours mean something.

Four colours, the same four in all three pictures. The colour tells you what kind of box you're looking at.

交出來之前，
它自己先看一眼。

線壓到字、兩個字擠在一起——這種毛病只有看圖才看得出來。它先看過，不對就重畫，然後才給你。

Before handing it over,
it looks at it.

A line running through a label, two words sitting on top of each other — you only ever see that in the picture. It looks first, redraws what's wrong, and hands it over after that.

一句話講得完的，
它不畫。

一個名詞、一個決定、一行字——直接講就好。要有好幾個步驟、中間會分岔，才值得畫。它拿不準的時候，會先問你一句：要圖，還是講一下就好？

If one sentence does it,
there's no picture.

One term, one decision, one line — you just get the answer. A picture is for something with several steps and a fork or two in the middle. When it can't tell, it asks you first: a diagram, or shall I just tell you?

你只要做兩件事。

說出你想弄懂的那一個東西。圖回來以後，把還是看不懂的那一格指給它看——它會把那一格重畫。

Two things for you to do.

Name the one thing you want to understand. When the picture comes back, point at the box that still doesn't make sense — it redraws that box.
