a question → one page

How the one-minute
explainer works

In one line: It finds the forks before it draws. A story told in a straight line has nowhere to put a fork, so the gates quietly disappear.
The three diagrams below are the whole thing.

圖一 · 從你問一句，到你拿到一頁

一道閘在最前面，一道在最後面，都不能跳過。

為什麼要這樣設計

兩道閘刻意擺在頭尾：小問題用講的比畫的快，排版壞掉只有圖上看得見。

Diagram 1 · From your question to your page

One gate at the front, one at the end. Neither of them is optional.

Why it works this way

The two gates sit at opposite ends on purpose: a small question is answered faster in words, and a layout fault only ever shows up in the picture.

圖二 · 真正改變結果的那個決定

不是畫得漂不漂亮，是岔路有沒有先找出來。

為什麼要這樣設計

一條線講到底沒地方放岔路，閘門不是看起來被漏掉、是看起來根本不存在；紅色用在只繞一下的路上，你會在流程其實沒停的地方停下來。

Diagram 2 · The decision that changes the result

Not how it looks. Whether the forks were found before anything was drawn.

Why it works this way

A straight line has nowhere to put a fork, so the gates do not look missing — they look as though they were never there; and red on a detour is the same mistake in miniature, because you stop where the flow does not.

圖三 · 這一頁要怎麼用

兩條路方向相反：你問過去，它做完再交回來。

為什麼要這樣設計

你只出一句話，跟後來那句「這張沒懂」；收窄、找岔路、自己先看都在它那邊。

Diagram 3 · How the page gets used

Two paths running opposite ways: yours goes over, its work comes back.

Why it works this way

You supply one line, and later one "this picture didn't land". The narrowing, the forks and the looking all sit on its side, so you never have to know how.

你可以指望的兩件事

錯了會被看見
  • 它交出來之前自己先看過：淺色深色各一次，中英文各一次
  • 線壓到框、兩個字疊在一起，只有看圖看得出來
三張圖是上限
  • 塞不下就是題目該切開，不是圖該擠更小
  • 兩頁窄的，好過一頁塞五張

哪一張沒講清楚，直接指那一張——那是重畫一張，不是重寫一頁。

Two things you can count on

A mistake gets seen
  • Before it reaches you, it has looked at the page itself — light and dark, and in both languages
  • A line over a box, two words sitting on top of each other: only the picture shows those
Three pictures is the ceiling
  • If it does not fit, the subject gets split — the pictures do not get squeezed smaller
  • Two narrower pages beat one page with five

If a picture does not land, point at that picture — that is one diagram redrawn, not a new page.
