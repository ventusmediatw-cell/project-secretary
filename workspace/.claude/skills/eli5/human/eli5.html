不懂的東西，
丟一句話過去。

打上 eli5，加上那個你看不懂的東西。你要做的就這樣。

Don't get it?
Send one line.

Type eli5, then the thing you don't get. That is the whole of your job.

它假設你
什麼都不懂。

同一件事，別人是寫給已經懂的人看的。這裡是重講一次，講給第一次聽到的人。

It assumes you
know nothing.

Everywhere else, the same thing is written for people who already understand it. Here it gets told again, to someone hearing it for the first time.

一頁。圖很大，
字很少。

圖是主角，字是配角。少掉的那些字是拿掉的，不是漏掉的。

One page. Big pictures,
few words.

The pictures do the work and the words help out. What isn't there was taken out, not left out.

它給的是第一眼看懂，
不是全部。

看完還想知道實際怎麼跑、什麼情況會走哪一條——那是另一種圖，再說一次就好。

It gets you to "oh, I see" —
not to the whole truth.

If you then want to know how it actually runs, and which way it goes when — that is a different kind of picture. Just ask again.

這個東西本身
只有一句話。

沒有步驟，沒有檢查表。多寫的每一條，都在把它變回那份你看不懂的說明書。

The thing itself
is one sentence.

No steps, no checklist. Every line anyone adds pulls it back towards the manual you couldn't read.

沒懂，就直接說。

「再簡單一點」「這張圖我看不懂」——它會重畫一次。看不懂不是你的問題。

If it didn't land, say so.

"Simpler." "I don't get this picture." It draws the whole thing again. Not understanding is not your fault.
