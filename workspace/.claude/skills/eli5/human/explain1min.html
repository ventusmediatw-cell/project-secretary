eli5

A skill that is
one sentence long

In one line: The whole skill is a single sentence — assume you know nothing, then allow only big pictures and very few words.
The three diagrams below are all of it.

圖一 · 從你開口到一頁圖

中間沒有步驟表。少掉的那些東西，就是它會動的原因。

為什麼要這樣設計

沒有檢查表，是因為兩個設定已經決定完了：讀者什麼都不懂、輸出是大圖少字。多一條規則，就多一件它忙著遵守、而不是忙著解釋的事。

Diagram 1 · From your one line to one page

There is no checklist in the middle. What is missing is what makes it work.

Why it works this way

There is no checklist because two settings already decide everything: the reader knows nothing, and the output is big pictures with few words. Every extra rule is one more thing to obey instead of explain.

圖二 · 真正改變結果的那個決定

兩個問題問完，該砍掉什麼就有答案了。

為什麼要這樣設計

「讀者已經知道多少」不是客套話，是刀。設成什麼都不懂，術語跟例外自己會掉；設成差不多懂，那些字會留著，你還是看不懂。

Diagram 2 · The decision that changes the result

Answer two questions and what to cut answers itself.

Why it works this way

"What does the reader know" is not a courtesy question, it is the knife. Set it to nothing and the jargon and the exceptions fall out on their own. Set it to roughly-informed and they stay in, and you still do not understand.

圖三 · 怎麼用它

你只出一句，設定那幾件事已經在裡面。

為什麼要這樣設計

差別不在它比較會寫，而在每次都一樣。長 prompt 每次重寫一遍，就每次漏掉不同的東西；一句話是同一組設定，第十次跟第一次同一種頁面。

Diagram 3 · How you use it

You supply one line. The settings are already inside it.

Why it works this way

The difference is not that it writes better, it is that it comes out the same every time. A long prompt gets rewritten on every use, so a different piece goes missing each time. One line is one fixed set of settings.

整支的兩條原則

薄是設計，不是還沒寫完
  • 沒有步驟、沒有角色、沒有範本，看起來像少寫了
  • 補上去，它就開始忙著遵守規則，不再忙著解釋東西
兩個旋鈕就夠了
  • 讀者是誰：什麼都不懂
  • 輸出長什麼樣：一頁 HTML、大圖、少字

還是卡住？把那一段指給它，它會再砍一次。

Two rules for the whole thing

Thin is the design, not an unfinished draft
  • No steps, no role, no template — it reads as if something is missing
  • Add them and it starts obeying rules instead of explaining things
Two dials are enough
  • Who the reader is — someone who knows nothing
  • What comes out — one HTML page, big pictures, few words

If a part of this page still does not land, point at that part — it cuts again.
